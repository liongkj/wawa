# Managed Wawa waitlist preparation

Wawa is self-hosted today. A managed service is a possible future offering; its availability, price and launch date are not yet decided. Do not publish a waitlist link until the form and its operator are ready.

## Suggested public copy

**Want Wawa hosted for you?**

We are exploring a managed version of Wawa. Join the waitlist to hear about it if it becomes available. There is no launch date or price yet.

**Email address**

**Join the waitlist**

Do not send passwords, WhatsApp messages or other sensitive information.

## Before publishing

- Choose an operator-controlled HTTPS form and a private destination accessible only to the people managing the waitlist.
- Collect only the email address. Provide a privacy notice identifying the operator, purpose, access, retention period and how to request deletion; decide those details before publishing.
- Decide how duplicate submissions and confirmation are handled, and ensure success and failure messages are clear.
- Send a clearly marked test submission, confirm it reaches the intended destination, and verify the operator can retrieve and delete it. Check keyboard access and a narrow mobile viewport.
- Add the verified link near the self-hosted introduction in the README. Consider an in-app link separately, and check it opens in the system browser on supported desktop platforms.
